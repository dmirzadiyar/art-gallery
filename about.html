Our story

Art belongs
to everyone.

Canvas & Co. is an independent gallery built around a simple belief: art can help us understand each other and see the world differently.

Why we exist

A place to pause, look, and feel.

We bring together established artists and emerging creators, giving their work room to speak. Our exhibitions mix painting, photography, sculpture, and experimental forms.

Whether you're visiting a gallery for the first time or already collect art, we want you to feel welcome, ask questions, and find something that resonates.
